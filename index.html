How an employee app became a hiring channel

Apple had started rejecting a retail app I led because it was for employees only. App Store reviews showed that people were downloading it to look for work. I proposed opening one part of it to everyone: a job application form that anyone could fill in. The rest of the app stayed for employees only. That was enough for Apple to treat the app as public and approve it. In the first three months, the retailer received about 20,000 applications through it.

How I work

  • Based in Belgrade, Serbia · Central European Time
  • EU and UK first. US East Coast mornings work too.
  • I work in English and Serbian.
  • I invoice in EUR from a registered business in Serbia.

  1. A paid first month.

    We agree the business problem, look at the evidence you already have, and set one priority with clear owners and a practical plan. Scope follows the access, people and budget you can actually give it.

  2. Then month by month.

    If it fits, I continue as your fractional CPO: priorities, trade-offs, dependencies across product, engineering and operations, and keeping every team tied to the goal you set. When the numbers say something should not be built, I say so. Days per week and scope are agreed individually.

  3. Checkpoints, not lock-in.

    Baseline and success criteria are set up front. At each review we continue, adjust the scope, or finish with a clean handover.

Who does what

I own
product priorities, trade-offs and delivery within the agreed budget. I track progress against your goal.
You own
the direction, business goal, budget and access. You decide when any of those change.
Your CTO and team own
technical quality, estimates and day-to-day execution.

Say our goal is to help more customers finish checkout. If payments are failing, I can move a dashboard request down the list so the team can fix checkout first. If we need a bigger budget or a different goal, that decision comes back to you.

Not included: setting your direction for you, unlimited development, round-the-clock availability, an agency team behind me, or guaranteed results.
