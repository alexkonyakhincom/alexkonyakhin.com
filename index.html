How I work

  1. A paid first month.

    We agree the business problem, look at the evidence you already have, and set one priority with clear owners and a practical plan. Scope follows the access, people and budget you can actually give it.

  2. Then month by month.

    If it fits, I continue as your fractional CPO: product direction, trade-offs, dependencies across product, engineering and operations, and keeping the work tied to the goal we agreed. Days per week and scope are agreed individually.

  3. Checkpoints, not lock-in.

    Baseline and success criteria are set up front. At each review we continue, adjust the scope, or finish with a clean handover.

Who does what

I own product direction, priorities, trade-offs, stakeholder alignment and measurement. You own the business goal, budget, access and decision authority. Your CTO and team own technical quality, estimates and day-to-day execution.

Not included: unlimited development, round-the-clock availability, an agency team behind me, or guaranteed results.
